A complete mix, performed without sight

File: features/blind_dj_workflow.feature

  • @manual
  • @e2e
  • @audio
  • @blocking

This is the acceptance test. Everything else in the plan checks a behaviour; this checks whether the behaviours add up to something a person can actually DJ with. Rules for running it: - Turn the monitor off, or close your eyes and keep them closed, or have someone else confirm you did not look. If you glance at the screen even once to recover from being lost, the run has failed — note where you looked and why, because that is the finding. - Do not use the mouse. - Do it at a realistic pace. Taking four minutes to find a track is a fail even if every announcement was correct, and that will not show up in any per-behaviour scenario. - Keep a stopwatch. Record how long the whole run took and where the time went. Run it twice: once keyboard-only, once with the DDJ-400. They exercise genuinely different paths and different failure modes. Key chords used below are the en_US defaults. Deck 1 is the left hand side of the keyboard, deck 2 the right.

Background

Every scenario below assumes these steps have already been done.

  1. Given a library containing at least 200 analysed tracks
  2. And headphones on a separate output from the main output
  3. And "Speech output" in Preferences, Accessibility is set to headphones
  4. And "Speak deck names as numbers" is enabled
  5. And "Smart cue" is enabled in Preferences, Decks
  6. And speech is on
  7. And I cannot see the screen

Scenario: A full mix from launch to filed track, keyboard only

  • @keyboard

Section: Getting in

  1. Given no DJ controller is connected
  2. When I launch Mixxx
  3. Then I hear "Mixxx ready"
  4. And I know from that alone that the audio device opened

Section: Finding the first track

  1. When I press "Tab" until I reach the search box
  2. And I type an artist name I know is in the library
  3. Then I hear how many tracks matched
  4. When I press "Escape" to move to the track list
  5. And I arrow through the results
  6. Then I hear each track's artist, title and position
  7. And I can tell the tracks apart well enough to choose one

Section: Loading and previewing

  1. When I press "Shift+Left" to load the selected track into deck 1
  2. Then I hear "Loaded deck, Alpha" followed by the artist, title, BPM and key
  3. And I hear the headphone cue move to deck 1 in the same announcement
  4. And I hear deck 1 in my headphones and nothing on the main output
  5. When I press "D" to start deck 1
  6. Then I hear it start playing
  7. When I press "Alt+1"
  8. Then I hear deck 1's full status: playing, time remaining, BPM and pitch

Section: Out to the room

  1. When I move the crossfader fully towards deck 1 using "H"
  2. Then deck 1 is audible on the main output
  3. When I press "T" to take deck 1 out of the headphones
  4. Then I hear "Deck 1 headphone cue off"

Section: Finding the next track by key and tempo

  1. When I press "Alt+5"
  2. Then I hear deck 1's BPM
  3. When I press "Alt+7"
  4. Then I hear deck 1's musical key
  5. When I search the library for a track and check its BPM and key from the row readout
  6. Then I can choose a track that will mix with deck 1 without seeing the screen

Section: Loading and cueing the second deck

  1. When I press "Shift+Right" to load it into deck 2
  2. Then I hear "Loaded deck, Bravo" with the artist, title, BPM and key
  3. And I hear the headphone cue move to deck 2
  4. And I hear deck 2 in my headphones while deck 1 continues on the main output

Section: Beatmatching by ear

  1. When I press "Alt+B" to turn the beat click on
  2. Then I hear "Beat click on"
  3. And I hear deck 1's click in my left ear
  4. When I press "Alt+S"
  5. Then I hear "Split cue on. Deck 1 left, deck 2 right"
  6. When I press "L" to start deck 2
  7. And I adjust deck 2's pitch with "F7" and "F8" until the two decks are in time
  8. Then I can hear the drift closing
  9. And I can hold them in time by ear alone
  10. When I press "Alt+6"
  11. Then I hear deck 2's BPM, close to deck 1's

Section: Setting up a mix point

  1. When I press "Shift+L" to set deck 2's cue point where I want the mix to start
  2. Then I hear the cue point confirmed
  3. When I press "Z" to jump to a hotcue on deck 1
  4. Then I hear deck 1 respond
  5. When I press "U" to set a beat loop on deck 2
  6. Then I hear the loop announced with its length
  7. When I press "I" to halve it
  8. Then I hear the new loop size
  9. When I press "O" to double it back
  10. Then I hear the loop size return to where it was
  11. When I press "9" to release the loop
  12. Then I hear the loop turn off

Section: The mix itself

  1. When I bring the crossfader across towards deck 2 using "G"
  2. Then I hear both tracks blending on the main output
  3. When I press "Ctrl+Alt+E" repeatedly to pull deck 1's bass out
  4. Then I hear the announcement name deck 1's EQ low and give me a value
  5. And deck 1's bass leaves the mix
  6. When I press "Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F11" to bring deck 2's bass up
  7. Then deck 2 carries the low end
  8. When I press "Ctrl+Alt+A" to switch effect unit 1 on
  9. Then I hear the unit announced as on
  10. And I hear the effect in the mix
  11. When I press "Ctrl+Alt+A" again
  12. Then the effect leaves the mix
  13. When I complete the crossfade to deck 2 using "G"
  14. And I press "Alt+3"
  15. Then I hear deck 1's time remaining, confirming it is safe to stop
  16. When I press "D" to stop deck 1
  17. Then I hear it stop

Section: Filing the track that worked

  1. When I press "Ctrl+Shift+C" to add deck 2's loaded track to a crate
  2. Then I hear the first crate name with its position
  3. When I arrow to the crate I want
  4. Then I hear each crate name and position as I go
  5. When I press "Return"
  6. Then I hear the track confirmed as added to that crate

Section: Handing over to Auto DJ

  1. When I add several tracks to the Auto DJ queue
  2. And I press "Shift+F12"
  3. Then I hear "Auto DJ on. Next: " followed by a track's artist and title
  4. When I press "Alt+Shift+N"
  5. Then I hear the Auto DJ state, the next track and the time remaining on the playing deck
  6. When I press "Shift+F11"
  7. Then I hear "Fading now"
  8. And the mix moves on without me touching anything

Section: Getting out

  1. When I press "Shift+F12"
  2. Then I hear "Auto DJ off"
  3. When I press "Alt+Shift+Left" while deck 1 is stopped
  4. Then I hear "Deck 1 track ejected"
  5. When I press "Ctrl+Q"
  6. Then Mixxx exits cleanly

Section: The verdict

  1. Then I performed the entire mix without looking at the screen
  2. And I never had to guess at the state of a deck
  3. And I was never left in silence wondering whether a key press registered
  4. And I record how long the run took and where I lost time

Scenario: Recovering from getting lost

  • @keyboard
  • @timing

Note: The realistic failure in a live set is not a wrong announcement, it is losing track of state. Every one of these must be answerable in one key press.

  1. Given both decks are loaded and one is playing
  2. And I have deliberately lost track of what is where
  3. When I press "Alt+1"
  4. Then I know whether deck 1 is playing, how long is left, its BPM and its pitch
  5. When I press "Alt+2"
  6. Then I know the same about deck 2
  7. When I press "Alt+Shift+T"
  8. Then I hear deck 1's artist and title
  9. When I press "Alt+Shift+Y"
  10. Then I hear deck 2's artist and title
  11. When I press "Alt+Shift+R"
  12. Then I hear the last announcement repeated
  13. Then I have recovered full awareness of the session in under 10 seconds

Scenario: Nothing destroys anything by accident during a set

  • @keyboard

Note: Run this at the end of a mix run, while tired and moving fast — that is when the accident happens.

  1. Given a mix is in progress with both decks loaded
  2. When I press "Return" repeatedly while focus is in the library
  3. Then no track is purged, hidden, removed or deleted
  4. When I open a track context menu and press "Escape"
  5. Then nothing has changed
  6. When I press "Alt+Shift+Left" while deck 1 is playing
  7. Then I hear "Deck 1 is playing, eject blocked. Stop the deck first."
  8. And deck 1 keeps playing
  9. And the mix is undisturbed throughout
